Introduction

I work on decision-making under uncertainty: bringing machine-learning forecasts into optimization models, designing supply chain networks, and building simulation and digital-twin tools for decision support. I graduated with First Class Honours in Industrial and Production Engineering from the University of Ibadan, ranked first of 48 in my department.

My research with the Climate Smart Decision Support Systems group at Michigan State University and at the University of Ibadan has appeared in The International Journal of Advanced Manufacturing Technology and AgriEngineering. I have also worked in AI engineering at Nethermind and in network deployment at Huawei, and I teach applied machine learning through the Huawei ICT Academy.

Research interests

{% include ill/optimization.svg %}

Optimization under uncertainty

Stochastic and robust models for decisions made before demand, lead times or supply are known.

{% include ill/learning.svg %}

Learning for decision-making

Predict-then-optimize and decision-focused learning, where forecasts are judged by the decisions they lead to.

{% include ill/logistics.svg %}

Logistics and supply chains

Network design, inventory control and coordination in urban last-mile logistics.

{% include ill/twin.svg %}

Simulation and digital twins

Decision support that tests a policy in a faithful model before it meets the real system.

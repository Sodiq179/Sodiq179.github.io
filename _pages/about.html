About

Industrial engineer and data scientist

Optimization, data analytics and machine learning for logistics and supply chains.

I am an industrial engineer and data scientist working at the intersection of optimization, data analytics and machine learning, with a focus on logistics and supply chains. I hold a B.Sc. in Industrial and Production Engineering from the University of Ibadan, where I graduated with First Class Honours, ranked first in my department.

My work centres on decision-making under uncertainty: bringing forecasts into optimization models, designing supply chain networks, and building simulation and digital-twin tools for decision support. I have carried out research with the Climate Smart Decision Support Systems group at Michigan State University and at the University of Ibadan, with publications in The International Journal of Advanced Manufacturing Technology and AgriEngineering.

Alongside research, I have worked in AI engineering at Nethermind and in network deployment at Huawei, and I teach applied machine learning through the Huawei ICT Academy. My work has been recognised with the Grand Prize at the Huawei ICT Competition Global Final and with First Prize in the Huawei ICT Global Teaching Competition in 2025 and 2026.

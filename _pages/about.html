About

Most important decisions are made before the data arrives. I work on making them well.

I work on decision-making under uncertainty: bringing machine-learning forecasts into optimization models, designing supply chain networks, and building simulation and digital-twin tools for decision support. I came to this through applied machine learning: my published work on computer vision for quality inspection and on digital twins for greenhouse monitoring made me most interested in the decisions such models inform. I graduated with First Class Honours in Industrial and Production Engineering from the University of Ibadan, ranked first of 48 in my department.

My research with the Climate Smart Decision Support Systems group at Michigan State University and at the University of Ibadan has appeared in The International Journal of Advanced Manufacturing Technology and AgriEngineering. I have also worked in AI engineering at Nethermind and in network deployment at Huawei, and I have taught applied machine learning through the Huawei ICT Academy. I am currently an independent researcher, working on optimization and learning methods for inventory control and energy-system planning.

Paper reproductions and extensions

Rebuilding published results from scratch, then pushing the method somewhere the authors didn't take it.

  • 2026 · with M. S. Jamal · reproduction of van der Haar, Wellens, Boute & Basten (2024), European Journal of Operational Research

    Supervised learning for integrated forecasting and lost-sales inventory control

    Optimality gap cut from up to 27% to 0.69% under stochastic lead times
    • Reproduced the lost-sales results at the paper's full training budget. Exact value iteration matched all 32 optimal costs on the Zipkin (2008) testbed, and the cost-trained LightGBM policy, using a custom gradient objective, came within 0.05 of the published cost in 28 of 32 instances.
    • Rebuilt the paper's confidential real-data study on the public M5 Walmart data. The learned policy was the cheapest of four methods in 11 of 16 settings.
    • Extension: designed a loss on the realised arrival period for stochastic lead times, cutting the gap to the optimal policy from up to 27% (fixed-lead-time model) to 0.69% on average, the best of all methods tested.
    • Inventory control
    • Dynamic programming
    • LightGBM
    • M5 data

  • 2026 · with M. Sammir · replication of Ruggles et al. (2024), Advances in Applied Energy

    Planning reliable wind- and solar-based electricity systems as a linear program

    12 of 13 capacities reproduced within 10% across 5,000+ LPs
    • Implemented the capacity-expansion and dispatch LP in Gurobi over 42 weather years in 4-hour steps (5,000+ LPs) and derived its dual. Reproduced the paper's hard and easy weather years exactly, with cost recovery from dual prices holding to machine precision.
    • Extensions: pricing lost load in planning lowered tested total cost by up to 26%, and cycling hydrogen storage within each year shrank the store by 27% for 1.3% more cost.
    • Linear programming
    • Duality
    • Gurobi
    • Energy systems

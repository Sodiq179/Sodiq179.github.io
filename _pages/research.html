Reproductions and extensions

I rebuild recent operations research papers from scratch, check every published number I can, then take the method somewhere the authors did not.

2026 · with M. S. Jamal · van der Haar, Wellens, Boute & Basten (2024), European Journal of Operational Research

Supervised learning for integrated forecasting and lost-sales inventory control

32/32
optimal costs matched exactly on the Zipkin (2008) testbed
11/16
real-data settings on M5 Walmart data where the learned policy was cheapest
0.69%
average gap to optimal with my new loss under stochastic lead times, down from 3.89%

Reproduced

  • Exact value iteration matched all 32 optimal costs. The cost-trained LightGBM policy, with a custom gradient objective, came within 0.05 of the published cost in 28 of 32 instances.
  • Rebuilt the paper's confidential real-data study on the public M5 Walmart data.

Extended

  • Designed a loss on the realised arrival period for stochastic lead times. The average gap fell from 3.89% to 0.69% and the worst case from 27% to 1.39%, the best of all methods tested.
  • Inventory control
  • Dynamic programming
  • LightGBM
  • M5 data
Code and report

2026 · with M. Sammir · Ruggles et al. (2024), Advances in Applied Energy

Planning reliable wind- and solar-based electricity systems as a linear program

5,000+
linear programs over 42 weather years in 4-hour steps, solved in Gurobi
12/13
published capacities reproduced within 10%, with cost recovery from duals to machine precision
−26%
tested total cost when lost load is priced in planning, my first extension

Reproduced

  • Implemented the capacity-expansion and dispatch LP and derived its dual. Reproduced the paper's hard and easy weather years exactly.

Extended

  • Pricing lost load in planning lowered tested total cost by up to 26%.
  • Cycling hydrogen storage within each year shrank the store by 27% for 1.3% more cost.
  • Linear programming
  • Duality
  • Gurobi
  • Energy systems
